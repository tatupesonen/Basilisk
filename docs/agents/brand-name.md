## The change (commit 3)

- `tools/word.py`: a new `B` glyph replaces `b` as the first letter. It has
  a 16-unit stroke with butt ends, a stem from y 0 to 140 (as tall as the
  l and k, on the same baseline), and two bowls, each a bar plus a half
  circle: upper r 29, lower r 33, with the middle bar at y 66 and the right
  edges at 86 and 92. The gap to the a is 14, as it was from the b. Colour
  handling is unchanged: the B is part of the single ink stroke path
  (`currentColor` on the site).

  units narrower than the b (5.9 viewBox units), so the original formula

  clipping. The logo `aria-label` now reads `Basilisk`.

  and `png/preview-logo-dark.png` (still 1400×505), and
  `png/preview-overview.png` (still 1600×1104). All other SVGs and PNGs came
  out byte-identical.

  replaced with the new ones. The emblem, viewBox and colours are
  unchanged.
- Alt text `basilisk` → `Basilisk` on the logo image in the root
